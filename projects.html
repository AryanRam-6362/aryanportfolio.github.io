Collaborative Robotic Workcell for Multi-Stage Quality Assurance in Luxury Watch Production

Engineered and empirically validated an Industry 4.0 collaborative robotic workcell in partnership with Timex Group Luxury Division and SUPSI DTI Omnifactory. The system pairs an ABB GoFa (CRB 15000) cobot with a Mech-Mind 3D camera and custom vacuum EOAT to automate pneumatic water-resistance machine tending (Check 2) and optical surface inspection (Check 6). Pioneered an unsupervised Dual-Engine vision pipeline combining sliding-window PatchCore with 2D Fast Fourier Transform notch filtering, slashing curved sapphire false alarms from 32.0% to 2.0% with 96.0% recall. Features an event-sourced SQLite WAL ledger, client-side OpenXML BOM ingestion, and a conversational AI Quality Analyst, accelerating tray throughput by 5.0× with a 5.3-month CapEx amortization.
